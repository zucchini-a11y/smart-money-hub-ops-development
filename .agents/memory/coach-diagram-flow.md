name: Coach diagram flow
description: User-approved diagram and learning-resource behavior in private Coach chats.
---

In private Coach chats, send a matching diagram immediately when someone asks for it or explicitly says they are confused. For a matching concept-learning question without either signal, offer the diagram in plain text and wait for an affirmative reply. Do not offer diagrams on every mention of a topic, chart follow-up, or setup discussion. When a matching saved image exists, do not claim the bot cannot display images or substitute an ASCII drawing. Never use an inline diagram button. Keep PDFs and videos out of the first answer; provide their links only when the person asks for a resource or says the explanation is unclear.

**Why:** the user chose all three pathways and rejected a diagram button and unsolicited learning-resource attachments. They also reported that a text-only liquidity sketch was confusing when the bot can send the saved visual, and requested uploaded chart-photo analysis for the owner only.

**How to apply:** keep diagrams private and topic-matched. A plain-text “yes” accepts the current diagram offer; a “no” clears it. Do not send the diagram to a group or channel. Only the configured owner may upload chart photos for analysis, only in the owner's private chat; never download group photos or store uploaded image bytes. This owner-only upload rule does not prevent sending saved, topic-matched diagrams privately to members.
