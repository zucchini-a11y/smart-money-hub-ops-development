---
name: Coach diagram flow
description: User-approved diagram and learning-resource behavior in private Coach chats.
---

In private Coach chats, send a matching diagram immediately when someone asks for it or explicitly says they are confused. For a matching concept answer without either signal, offer the diagram in plain text and wait for an affirmative reply. Never use an inline diagram button. Keep PDFs and videos out of the first answer; provide their links only when the person asks for a resource or says the explanation is unclear.

**Why:** the user chose all three pathways and rejected a diagram button and unsolicited learning-resource attachments.

**How to apply:** keep diagrams private and topic-matched. A plain-text “yes” accepts the current diagram offer; a “no” clears it. Do not send the diagram to a group or channel.
