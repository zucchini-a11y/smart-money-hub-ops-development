---
name: Coach chat reactions
description: User-approved scope and intent for automatic reactions to member messages.
---

The Coach may react to meaningful member messages in both private Coach chats and the Education group. Keep reactions selective: acknowledge reported learning progress with 👍 and disciplined trading-process choices with 🎯. Do not react to every message, routine questions, or commands. Reactions are optional; Telegram permission or chat-setting failures must not block a reply.

**Why:** The user asked the bot to like member messages when they understand something or share another meaningful contribution, and chose both private chats and the Education group.

**How to apply:** Use reactions to acknowledge learning progress or process discipline, not to validate a trade outcome, entry signal, or technical claim as correct.
