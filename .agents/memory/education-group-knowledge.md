The Smart Money Hub education group is a forum-style supergroup where ordinary members currently cannot send regular text messages. The bot is an administrator and can observe incoming posts; leave the existing member permissions and forum topics unchanged unless the user explicitly requests a change. Members who cannot post in the group must use the private Coach for conversational questions.

Telegram does not provide the bot with arbitrary access to older group history. Build awareness from approved knowledge and posts received after archival is enabled; do not claim the archive is complete. Keep group-derived public context separate from each member's private Coach history and journals.

**Why:** the user asked for group-aware coaching while explicitly preserving the group’s current permissions and topics; Telegram only supplies updates the bot receives.

**How to apply:** before promising natural group Q&A or complete historical coverage, confirm members can post and explain that only archived/approved material is searchable. Do not change BotFather privacy settings or group permissions as a workaround.
