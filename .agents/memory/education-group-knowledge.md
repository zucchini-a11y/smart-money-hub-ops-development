description: Telegram group posting permissions and history limits that affect Smart Money Hub group-aware coaching.

The Smart Money Hub education group is a forum-style supergroup where ordinary members currently cannot send regular text messages. The bot is an administrator and can observe incoming posts; leave the existing member permissions and forum topics unchanged unless the user explicitly requests a change. Members who cannot post in the group must use the private Coach for conversational questions.

Telegram does not provide the bot with arbitrary access to older group history. Build awareness from approved knowledge and posts received after archival is enabled; do not claim the archive is complete. Keep group-derived public context separate from each member's private Coach history and journals.

Automatic Education polls run weekly on Saturday at 9:00 PM WAT. Channel posts should be clearly labeled as previews and link to the real Education-group poll with a separate voting button. Only the configured administrator can request an extra poll through private chat text.

**Why:** the user chose this cadence and channel-preview format, and wants on-demand poll posting without approving each scheduled poll.

**How to apply:** retain the weekly cadence and preview/button distinction, keep ad-hoc requests private-admin-only, and never change member permissions or poll destinations as a workaround.

**Why:** the user asked for group-aware coaching while explicitly preserving the group’s current permissions and topics; Telegram only supplies updates the bot receives.

**How to apply:** before promising natural group Q&A or complete historical coverage, confirm members can post and explain that only archived/approved material is searchable. Do not change BotFather privacy settings or group permissions as a workaround.

The daily Education-group morning post is scheduled for 08:00 WAT. If the bot recovers within 30 minutes of that time, it may send the scheduled post once after the normal destination and permission checks. After the 30-minute window, record the post as skipped and do not send it late.

**Why:** the user requested a 30-minute recovery window while still avoiding stale late posts.

**How to apply:** keep the scheduled time unchanged, use the 30-minute boundary consistently, prevent duplicates, and do not reset an already-skipped run to trigger a late send.

Daily Education lessons should change subject, not only wording; avoid repeating a subject across recent morning lessons when another approved subject is available.

**Why:** the user chose daily lesson subjects as the thing that must change.

**How to apply:** rotate across distinct lesson topics and do not repeat a recent topic merely to produce different wording.

Weekend cleanup is limited to the bot's tracked Saturday and Sunday morning lessons and Saturday Education-group poll, scheduled for Sunday at 11:00 PM WAT. Leave earlier-week lessons, member messages, and unrelated bot posts untouched.

**Why:** the user explicitly selected this cleanup scope and time after the Telegram 48-hour deletion limit was explained.

**How to apply:** delete only those tracked group messages; do not expand cleanup to other destinations or content types.
