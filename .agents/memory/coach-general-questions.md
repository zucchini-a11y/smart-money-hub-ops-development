description: Scope for the Smart Money Hub Coach beyond trading education.

The user wants the Coach to answer their ordinary questions across topics, not redirect them to SMC lessons by default. Preserve useful partial answers and never say an administrator was notified unless a real notification was sent. The Coach must still be clear when a question requires live data or other information it cannot access. Treat brief “why” and similar follow-ups as referring to the immediately previous answer even when that answer did not end with a question; do not jump to an older lesson.

**Why:** The user asked for the bot to be available in case they want to ask anything and objected to generic, unhelpful verification and administrator-review wording. A bare “why” after a posting limitation was answered with an unrelated retest lesson when the previous reply was not a question.

**How to apply:** Keep Gemini Coach prompts open to general knowledge, writing, technology, planning, and everyday questions. Keep live market, channel, news, and changing platform facts bounded by evidence and available integrations.
