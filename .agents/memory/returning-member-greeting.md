---
name: Returning-member greeting
description: The Coach's response when someone returns after a period of inactivity.
---

After at least 30 minutes of inactivity, send a brief professional welcome-back message. After an hour, use a slightly warmer version. Do not guess why the person was away, and do not interrupt an active quiz or coaching flow.

**Why:** The user wants the bot to recognize that they may be busy and welcome them back professionally when they return.

**How to apply:** Keep the acknowledgement concise, separate from the actual response, and preserve the current session or requested action.
