---
name: Member announcement permissions
description: Role boundaries for staff-created announcements delivered to member accounts.
---

Managers may create and edit their own announcement drafts, but only Owners may publish a platform-wide member announcement. Revisit this boundary only after a verified member-to-team relationship exists and manager recipients can be restricted to their authorized teams.

**Why:** Staff Managers are team-scoped, while member accounts currently have no verified mapping to those staff teams. Letting a Manager broadcast to all members would exceed their established scope.

**How to apply:** For member-wide announcements, keep Owner publication as the server-enforced permission. If team-scoped member announcements are added later, derive recipients from verified membership data rather than trusting a client-supplied audience.
