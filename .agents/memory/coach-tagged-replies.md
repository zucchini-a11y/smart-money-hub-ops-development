---
name: Private Coach tagged replies
description: Scope and behavior for mentioning members in Coach replies.
---

The Coach should reply directly to a member's incoming message and tag the member in private Coach chats only. This applies to text conversations, guided Coach answers, and private chart-image replies; group replies remain untagged.

**Why:** The user selected private Coach chats only.

**How to apply:** Keep reply-to and mention behavior limited to private Coach responses. Do not add it to public Education-group answers or unrelated admin messages.
