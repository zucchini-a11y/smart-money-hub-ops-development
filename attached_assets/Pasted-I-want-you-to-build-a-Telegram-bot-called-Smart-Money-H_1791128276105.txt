3. TRADING JOURNAL

Create a Trading Journal button.

When a member selects it, ask:

1. What pair/asset did you trade?
2. Buy or sell?
3. What was your POI?
4. What timeframe?
5. What confirmation did you see?
6. Was liquidity swept?
7. Did you wait for a retest?
8. Where was your stop loss?
9. Where was your TP?
10. Did you take partial profit?
11. Did you move SL to BE?
12. What was the result?
13. Did you follow your plan?
14. What mistake did you make, if any?
15. What did you learn?

4. TRADE REVIEW

After a member journals a trade, give them a structured review.

Direction: Sell
POI: 15M FVG
Confirmation: Rejection wick
Liquidity sweep: No
Retest: No
Result: Loss

You entered before sufficient confirmation.

Reminder:
No confirmation = no entry.

Do not insult or shame the member.

The purpose is education and discipline, not predicting trades.
