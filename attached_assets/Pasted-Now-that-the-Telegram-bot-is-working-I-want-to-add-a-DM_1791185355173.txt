### DM COACH

When a member sends a normal text message to the bot, the bot should respond as a Smart Money Hub trading coach.

The coach should understand and use my trading framework:

* Market Structure
* BOS
* CHOCH
* MSS
* Liquidity
* SSL / BSL
* Order Blocks
* FVG / IFVG
* POI
* Displacement
* Retest
* Confirmation
* HTF / LTF analysis
* Risk management
* Partial profits
* Break-even
* Trade psychology
* Trading discipline

My core rules are:

"No confirmation, no entry."

"No retest, no entry."

"Don't chase missed trades."

"Risk management first."

The coach should teach members to understand their own setups rather than simply giving them entries.

### IMPORTANT

For now, do NOT connect any paid AI API.

Build the DM system so the AI provider can be added later through a separate service such as:

`ai_evaluate_answer()`

The current version can use predefined responses, keyword/topic detection, and guided questions.

### EXAMPLES

Member:
"What is CHOCH?"

Bot:
Explain CHOCH simply, then ask a short question to check whether the member understands it.

Member:
"I saw an FVG and entered immediately."

Bot:
Explain that an FVG alone isn't necessarily confirmation and ask what happened with liquidity, displacement and retest.

Member:
"I got stopped out."

Bot:
Ask:

1. What was your POI?
2. What timeframe?
3. What liquidity was taken?
4. What confirmation did you see?
5. Did price retest?
6. Where was your SL?
7. Did you follow your plan?

Then help identify the mistake.
